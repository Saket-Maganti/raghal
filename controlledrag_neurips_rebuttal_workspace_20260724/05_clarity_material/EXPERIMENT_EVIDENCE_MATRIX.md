# Experiment Evidence Matrix

## Reading rule

“Submitted” means present in the immutable reviewer artifact. “Recovered”
means a fixed output traced and hashed in Prompt 02 but not silently relabeled
as submitted. “Aggregated” means Prompt 03 computed statistics from fixed
rows. Evidence grades describe this rebuttal package, not universal validity.
`NR` means the historical artifact did not report or lock the field.

## Human-protocol qualifier

The two human raters labeled independently before reconciliation; agreement
uses pre-adjudication labels, while scorer alignment uses adjudicated labels.
The `n=99` and `n=100` slices have different sampling designs and label
schemas and are never pooled.

## Evidence boundary

No row above is a new model run. Recovered evidence is explicitly marked,
modern-judge evidence is unnecessary, and missing exact historical package or
model revisions prevent claims of bitwise scorer regeneration.
